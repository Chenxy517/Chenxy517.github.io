Ph.D. Student · Boston University

Hi, I'm Xingyu.

I study systems security and distributed systems, exploring how modern computing platforms leak information—and how to make them more efficient, reliable, and secure.

I'm a Ph.D. student in Computer Engineering at Boston University, working with Prof. Ari Trachtenberg. My research spans distributed networks, blockchain systems, data-efficient protocols, and side-channel security. I received my M.Eng. from Boston University and B.S. in Microelectronics from Fudan University.
